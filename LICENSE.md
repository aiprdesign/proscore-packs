## Concert Grand (ProScoro-piano-ConcertGrand-Salamander-part1.zip, -part2.zip): CC-BY 3.0
**Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed CC-BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).**
This is a Yamaha C5 grand. Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3 (also
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano).
Modified for ProScoro: stereo summed to mono, retuned, resampled 44.1 → 48 kHz, 8 of 16 velocity layers kept, tails
capped with a fade. "Felt / Soft" and "Film Ambient" are modelled presets of the same recordings.
This credit must be kept wherever the samples are shared. Legal code: [LICENSES/CC-BY-3.0.txt](LICENSES/CC-BY-3.0.txt).

City Piano by Big Cat Instruments, a Baldwin baby grand. Source:
https://bigcatinstruments.blogspot.com/2015/09/all-keyboard-instruments.html
The author released it to the public domain: "It is free and public domain. Do anything you like with it."
No attribution is required; we credit Big Cat Instruments as a courtesy.
Modified for ProScoro: mono, 48 kHz, every second key, tails capped with a fade, layer levels rebuilt.
