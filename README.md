### How to install the pianos
1. Download the zips you want. Packs with two parts need **both** parts.
2. Put them inside your `ProScoro-<version>` folder, extract them there, and say yes when asked to merge folders.
3. Restart ProScoro. It should say "Realistic pianos found".

### How to install the instruments
- **Easiest (in the app):** in ProScoro's Settings, set the sound-pack address to
  `https://github.com/aiprdesign/proscore-packs/releases/download/v1.1.0/`, then press **Install** next to a pack in
  **Installed instruments**. The app downloads it, checks the SHA-256, and installs it.
- **By hand:** download the zips, put them in your `ProScoro-<version>` folder (or your Downloads folder) and press
  **Install** in **Installed instruments**. The app finds the local copy and checks it. You can also extract them
  inside the `ProScoro-<version>` folder (merge folders) and restart.
- The orchestra pack has **7 parts**; get all 7.
- The Indian instruments (sitar, tanpura, santoor, shehnai, harmonium, sarangi) are not here: the app builds them itself on
  first start.
